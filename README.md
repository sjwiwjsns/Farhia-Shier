## Blaine, Minnesota — open-world driving simulation

A browser-playable 3D drive through a recreation of Blaine, MN: the real
arterial grid, the National Sports Center, Northtown, Blaine High School and the
north-side lakes, with dynamic weather, a day/night cycle, AI traffic and four
drivable vehicles. One self-contained HTML file, no external assets.

- Play: [`blaine/index.html`](blaine/index.html) (serve the repo and open `/blaine/`)
- Details and build instructions: [`blaine/README.md`](blaine/README.md)
